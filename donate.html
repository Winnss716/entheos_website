Why It Matters

Your Support Will
Directly Fund Care

Once operational, every dollar donated to the Entheos Veterans Project will go directly to veterans — with zero administrative overhead. We are building the foundation now so we can deliver real treatment, real scholarships, and real healing soon.

We serve U.S. military veterans and their families impacted by PTSD, traumatic brain injury (TBI), substance use disorders, and service-related psychological trauma.

Many veterans face barriers to effective care. We exist to help bridge that gap through innovative, evidence-based support.

Entheos Veterans Project is building pathways to healing for veterans facing PTSD, TBI, substance use disorders, and the invisible wounds of service.
