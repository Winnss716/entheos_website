Where Your Money Goes

Your Gift Directly
Funds Care

The Entheos Veteran Project operates with zero administrative overhead on donations. Every dollar you give is matched to a veteran in need and disbursed directly to treatment providers, schools, and recovery programs.

We believe the men and women who served this country deserve more than words — they deserve real resources, real treatment, and a real chance at reclaiming their lives.

$25
Covers one individual therapy session with a licensed clinician
$100
Funds one week of outpatient care or group counseling
$500
Covers one full month of treatment, rehabilitation, or coursework
$1,000+
Contributes to a full scholarship award for a veteran in critical need

The Entheos Veteran Project raises and distributes scholarship funds for veterans battling PTSD, TBI, substance abuse, and the invisible wounds of war.
