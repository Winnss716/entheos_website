Our Mission

No Veteran Fights
This Battle Alone

The Entheos Veteran Project exists to bridge the gap between service and healing. We raise and distribute scholarship funds that provide veterans with access to mental health treatment, educational advancement, and recovery programs — because those who served deserve every resource to reclaim their lives.

Who We Serve

The Invisible Wounds
We Fight to Heal

🧠

PTSD

Post-traumatic stress disorder affects millions of veterans. We fund access to trauma-focused therapy, EMDR, and evidence-based residential treatment programs.

Learn More →
⚡

Traumatic Brain Injury

TBI from blast exposure and combat creates lasting cognitive challenges. Our scholarships cover neurological rehabilitation, cognitive therapy, and long-term care.

Learn More →
🔗

Substance Abuse

Many veterans self-medicate invisible wounds. We provide funding for inpatient rehab, outpatient counseling, sober living programs, and addiction recovery support.

Learn More →
💙

Mental Health & Depression

Depression, anxiety, and moral injury follow service home. We connect veterans to licensed clinicians, group therapy, and holistic wellness resources.

Learn More →
🎓

Education & Reintegration

Transitioning back to civilian life is its own battle. We fund vocational training, college tuition, and career-development programs for healing veterans.

Learn More →
🤝

Family Support

Healing doesn't happen in isolation. We support the families of veterans with counseling resources and financial assistance during treatment periods.

The Scholarship

How We Fund
Your Healing

The Entheos Scholarship is awarded to veterans who demonstrate financial need and are actively pursuing healing and recovery. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis with no deadline.

01

Complete the Application

Fill out our secure online form with basic service history, current needs, and treatment goals.

02

Review & Verification

Our team reviews your application within 7–10 business days. We may contact you for additional information.

03

Funds Disbursed

Approved funds are sent directly to treatment providers, schools, or programs on your behalf.

Start Your Application
Every Case Individually Assessed

There is no flat rate — each scholarship is carefully reviewed and tailored to the veteran's unique situation. Healing is not one-size-fits-all, and neither is our support. No cost to apply. No repayment. Ever.
