    The Entheos Network

    Our Partners

    Every partner in the Entheos network is carefully vetted and aligned with our commitment to veteran healing — no shortcuts, no one-size-fits-all, no compromises on quality of care.

    We don't simply refer veterans to treatment. We build a curated ecosystem of preparation coaches, proven treatment facilities, soft-landing programs, and integration coaches — each selected for their depth of experience with the veteran population and their alignment with our whole-person approach to healing.

    Category 01

    Preparation Coaches

    The quality of preparation before treatment is one of the strongest predictors of outcomes. Our preparation coaches work one-on-one with veterans in the weeks before treatment — building psychological readiness, establishing trust, setting intentions, and creating the relational foundation that makes healing possible.

    Category 02

    Providers & Treatment Facilities

    We partner exclusively with treatment providers who meet rigorous standards of medical safety, clinical integrity, and veteran cultural competence. Each facility is personally reviewed by our team — never chosen by convenience or referral fees. Our veterans deserve nothing less than the most qualified, experienced care available.

    Category 03

    Aftercare & Soft Landing

    The 10–14 days following treatment are where outcomes are often won or lost. Our soft-landing partners provide structured, gentle environments — nature, rest, nourishing food, and quiet community — that protect the veteran's open and vulnerable state and allow the experience to integrate before re-entering the demands of everyday life.

    Category 04

    Integration Coaches

    Transformation doesn't end at the facility door — it begins there. Our integration coaches walk alongside veterans for months after treatment, helping them make meaning of their experience, apply insights to daily life, navigate relationships, rebuild identity, and build the long-term structures that protect and deepen their healing over time.
