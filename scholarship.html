The Process

Simple. Fast.
No Red Tape.

1

Complete the Application

Fill out our secure online form with your basic service history, current needs, and treatment goals. Takes about 10 minutes. No cost, no commitment.

2

Review & Verification

Our team carefully reviews every submission within 7–10 business days. We may contact you for additional information or to connect you with resources while you wait.

3

Approval Notification

You'll be notified by email or phone. Approved applicants receive a formal award letter and are contacted to confirm treatment program details.

4

Funds Disbursed Directly

Payment goes straight to your provider, school, or program. You never handle the money — there's nothing to repay, ever.

Every Case Individually Assessed

There is no flat rate or average — every scholarship is reviewed with care and tailored to that veteran's specific situation. We understand that healing is not one-size-fits-all, and funding reflects each individual's unique path.

Eligibility

Who Qualifies

You May Qualify If…

★

You are a U.S. military veteran of any branch and any era of service

★

You are actively seeking or enrolled in a treatment, recovery, or education program

★

You have a demonstrated financial need that creates a barrier to receiving care

★

You are dealing with PTSD, TBI, substance abuse, mental health challenges, or reintegration difficulties

★

Family members or caregivers of qualifying veterans may also be eligible for support services

What to Have Ready

◆

Basic service history — branch, years served, discharge status

◆

Description of your current situation — what you're facing and what care you're seeking

◆

Name of treatment provider or program you intend to use (if known)

◆

Estimated cost of care or tuition (approximate is fine)

◆

A valid email address or phone number so we can follow up
