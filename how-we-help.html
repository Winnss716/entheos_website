Our Approach

A Clear Path Forward

We know how overwhelming it can feel trying to find real help after everything you’ve been through. We’ve been in your boots. That’s why we built a straightforward, veteran-led pathway that starts with you — your story, your pace, and your choices.

We Start With You

We Meet You Where You Are

We don’t expect you to have it all figured out. We know how confusing and exhausting it can be trying to navigate treatment options, systems, and what comes next. We’ve lived it ourselves — the CPTSD before service, the moral injury, the TBI, the hypervigilance that doesn’t switch off, and the difficulty of actually coming home.

That’s why we begin by listening. Really listening. We take the time to understand your unique story, what you’ve been through, and what you actually need — not what some brochure says you should need.

How We Help

What Working With Us Looks Like

We walk the entire path with you. Here’s the general shape of how we support veterans and their families:

1. Getting to Know You

We start with real conversation — no forms first, no pressure. We learn your story, your challenges, and what matters most to you and your family.

2. Preparation That’s Built Around You

Together we create a plan that fits your life. We give you options and help you understand what to expect, so you feel prepared instead of thrown in.

3. Treatment as a Threshold

Ibogaine is one powerful part of the journey — not the whole story. We support you through it with experienced care and a clear plan for what comes next.

4. The Soft Landing

The days and weeks right after treatment are critical. We make sure you have space to rest, reconnect, and settle — without being rushed into heavy processing.

5. Ongoing Support That Actually Continues

This doesn’t end when treatment ends. We stay in relationship with you through integration, check-ins, crisis support when needed, and long-term connection — including scholarship support to help remove financial barriers.

You Stay in Control

This Is Your Journey — Not Ours

We believe you should never feel like you’re being pushed through a system. At every stage we give you real options and help you make informed choices. You decide the pace. You decide what feels right for you and your family.

We’re here to help you navigate the complicated parts — the paperwork, the unknowns, the systems that weren’t built for veterans — so you don’t have to figure it all out alone. You stay in the driver’s seat. We just walk beside you.

The Support That Actually Matters

Relational Holding & The Soft Landing

We know how important it is to feel seen and respected as a whole person — not just another case. That’s why we treat relationship as the foundation, not an add-on. We build trust before treatment even begins, stay in real contact throughout, and bring real experience when things get hard.

The 10–14 days after treatment are when things often feel most uncertain. We deliberately create space for rest, nature, good food, gentle connection, and time to just be — without forcing heavy emotional work. We’ve seen how much this matters, and we make sure it’s built into the path.

Entheos Veterans Project is building pathways to healing for veterans facing PTSD, TBI, substance use disorders, and the invisible wounds of service.
