Our Approach

A Pathway,
Not Just Treatment

We don’t simply provide ibogaine treatment. We deliver a veteran-specific, end-to-end pathway designed to make healing actually last — from preparation through long-term integration.

The Entheos Difference

We Walk the Path With You

Most organizations fund treatment, facilitate access, and maybe add some integration support afterward. We do something fundamentally different.

We design complete transformation architecture. This includes deep psychological preparation, identity work (soldier → human being), relational trust building, a true “soft landing,” and structured long-term integration. Ibogaine is a powerful threshold — but the pathway around it determines whether transformation takes root.

Specialists in the Population Others Misunderstand

We understand CPTSD layered before service, moral injury, TBI, hypervigilance as identity, and the profound difficulty of “coming home.” Ibogaine rarely shows veterans the war — it often reveals the architecture that led them there. That insight changes everything.

Ibogaine as Threshold, Not Cure

We reject the “reset,” “one-and-done,” or “interrupt addiction” framing. Ibogaine doesn’t heal you. It invites you to heal yourself — and we walk that path with you. This filters the wrong expectations and builds long-term credibility.

Built from Lived Experience

This pathway comes from what actually happens after treatment — not what brochures promise before it. We’ve seen what works, what fails, and how to manage the real downstream challenges veterans face.

Measurable Integration

The Good Life Index

Healing is not vague. We use a structured, repeatable integration framework with measurable domains that serve as a compass before and after treatment. This is far more than therapy check-ins — it is a philosophy, framework, and tracking system that makes progress visible and accountable.

01

Identity & Belonging

Reclaiming a sense of self beyond the uniform. Rebuilding connection to family, community, and purpose.

02

Emotional Regulation

Reducing hypervigilance and reactivity. Building capacity to feel without being overwhelmed.

03

Relational Health

Restoring trust in relationships. Learning to receive support and set healthy boundaries.

04

Meaning & Purpose

Rediscovering authorship over one’s life story. Moving from survival to contribution.

05

Physical & Nervous System

Supporting the body’s recovery from chronic stress and TBI. Restoring sleep, energy, and safety in the body.

06

Long-Term Stability

Creating sustainable structures — routines, community, and practices — that protect gains over time.

What Most Programs Miss

Relational Holding & The Soft Landing

If the individual is not seen, heard, acknowledged, and respected — the treatment is compromised. We treat relational holding as non-negotiable, not an add-on. That means pre-treatment relationship building, ongoing contact (not session-bound), and real crisis management experience.

The 10–14 days after treatment are critical. We deliberately create space for nature, rest, nourishing food, gentle community, and play — with no heavy processing. This is where outcomes are often won or lost. We don’t just identify this gap; we actively fill it.

Language Veterans Trust

Speaking the Language of Mission & Homecoming

We don’t speak in clinical abstraction or psychedelic evangelism. We speak in the language of mission, reconnaissance, brotherhood, and homecoming. This creates authentic cultural translation — from traditional healing wisdom to the military psyche — that veterans can actually hear and trust.

Entheos Veterans Project is building pathways to healing for veterans facing PTSD, TBI, substance use disorders, and the invisible wounds of service.
